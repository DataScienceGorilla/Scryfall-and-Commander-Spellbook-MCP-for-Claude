What it is

  • A sounding board for your brewing process
  • A way to find cards you may not have considered
  • A diagnostic for potential issues in your list
  • A method for refinement

What it isn't

  • A shortcut for thinking through a strategy
  • Always correct
  • A one-button deck generator

What it can be

  • A deckbuilding assistant that knows your habits and shortcomings while deckbuilding
  • A means of evaluating the strength of a list and the bracket it belongs in
  • A coach for better deckbuilding
